Failure Points, UI & System Responses

OTP delivery interrupts new-payee authorization

TC03 · DEF-101 · Critical

Observed system response: The OTP was not received within 60 seconds.

User impact: The user may be unable to authorize and complete a transfer to a new payee during the tested window.

Automatic retry can result in a duplicate debit

TC06 · DEF-102 · Major

Observed system response: A transfer during network loss was debited twice after an automatic retry.

User impact: This creates a transaction-integrity risk. The related transfer-history test, TC13, is blocked until DEF-102 is fixed, so its behavior remains unverified.

Small-screen layout hides the confirmation action

TC09 · DEF-103 · Minor

Observed UI behavior: The confirm button is hidden on 5-inch screens.

User impact: Users on affected devices may not be able to reach the final confirmation step.

Validation feedback is inconsistent

TC12 · DEF-104 · Major; TC04 · Pass

Observed system response: For an invalid beneficiary account number, the app shows a generic error instead of a field-level message. In contrast, TC04 passed and its note records that the daily-limit error was shown correctly.

User impact: On the invalid-account path, users receive less guidance about what to correct than on the tested daily-limit path.

Peak-load response exceeds the target

TC15 · DEF-105 · Major

Observed system response: Under a load of 500 users, response time exceeded the 5-second target; the test execution time recorded in the CSV is 12.4 seconds.

User impact: Transfers may feel slow under the tested peak-load condition.

User impacts above are interpretations of the recorded test observations, not additional test results.

Strategic Recommendations

Ranked by potential customer and financial impact, then by the severity and reach of the observed issue.

  1. Priority P0 · Urgent

    Guarantee one debit outcome across retries

    Make transfer retries idempotent and preserve an authoritative transaction state when connectivity is interrupted. Reconcile uncertain outcomes before allowing another debit or presenting completion to the user.

    Validate: Repeat requests before and after transaction commit; each transfer must produce at most one debit and one canonical result. Re-run the blocked transfer-history check after the fix.

    Addresses DEF-102 (TC06); unblocks TC13.

  2. Priority P0 · Urgent

    Make OTP delivery reliable without weakening authorization

    Instrument OTP issuance, delivery, and verification end to end; give users a clear wait and recovery path with bounded resend, expiry, and attempt controls. Do not treat a transfer as authorized until valid verification succeeds.

    Validate: Exercise delayed and failed delivery, resend, expiration, and repeated-code attempts. Confirm that recovery is understandable and unauthorized transfers cannot proceed.

    Addresses DEF-101 (TC03, Critical).

  3. Priority P1 · High

    Meet the response-time target under peak load

    Profile the transfer path at the tested 500-user load, identify the dominant bottleneck, and optimize it while monitoring timeouts and failed transactions alongside latency.

    Validate: Re-run the 500-user scenario and meet the existing 5-second response-time target; report latency distribution and transaction success rate.

    Addresses DEF-105 (TC15).

  4. Priority P1 · High

    Make beneficiary validation specific and recoverable

    Show an actionable message next to the invalid account field, retain the entered form state, and prevent submission until the value is corrected. Keep validation behavior consistent with the correctly displayed daily-limit response.

    Validate: Re-run invalid beneficiary, daily-limit, and insufficient-balance cases; each response should identify the issue without creating a transfer.

    Addresses DEF-104 (TC12); regression checks TC04 and TC05.

  5. Priority P2 · Planned

    Keep confirmation visible and usable on small screens

    Revise the confirmation layout so the primary action remains visible or reachable by scrolling, with the amount and payee details still clear before confirmation.

    Validate: Check the supported device and text-size range, including the 5-inch case, and confirm the action works with touch and accessibility navigation.

    Addresses DEF-103 (TC09).

Security-related recommendations are tied to the OTP authorization and duplicate-debit findings. The source CSV records no defect in the Security module; TC11 (session timeout) passed.
